How hard is it raining near my creek?

We compare rain in the hills above each creek with how often rain that heavy happens there. The lower the chance in a given year, the rarer the downpour — and the more likely creeks rise fast.

What the colors mean

Same levels as the public page. Each creek takes the heaviest rain at any of its rain gauges. Chance in any year is the annual exceedance probability (AEP) of that rain. Severe needs at least two gauges agreeing.

CreekLevelChance in any yearHeaviest rainGauges elevatedNotes

This shows rain, not flooding. A creek can flood with less rain when the ground is already soaked, and a heavy downpour does not always cause flooding.

“10% chance in any year” means rain this heavy is expected about once a decade on average — but it can happen two years in a row.

Thresholds: NOAA Atlas 14 Vol. 6 v2 point precipitation frequency estimates (partial-duration series) at each gauge; AEP = 1 − e−1/ARI. Never walk or drive through moving water. In an emergency, call 911.
