How to read this page

Creek level

Measured in the creek on creeks that have a gauge. Watch (orange frame): the creek is rising toward flooding. Warning (red frame): flooding is likely or happening.

These are separate from National Weather Service alerts. Follow whichever is more serious.

Rain upstream

How unusual the rain is in the hills above the creek. More bars means heavier rain: Heavy, Very heavy, Severe. Cards with severe rain have a bold black frame.

Rain is not the same as flooding, but heavy rain can make creeks rise quickly.

Tide

For creeks near the Bay. When the tide is rising, water drains more slowly and low areas can flood more easily.
