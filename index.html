How to read this page

The status at the top of each card

  • Flooding likely — move away from the creek and low areas now.
  • Be ready — the creek could flood. Be ready to move to higher ground.
  • Stay alert — conditions are worsening. Avoid creek banks and check back often.
  • No alerts — nothing unusual right now. Conditions can change quickly.

Why a creek has its status

Some creeks have flood levels set: Monitor, Watch and Warning, shown on a bar with a marker at the current level.

Every creek also shows whether the water is rising or falling, how heavy the rain is in the hills above it, and — near the Bay — whether a rising tide is slowing drainage.
